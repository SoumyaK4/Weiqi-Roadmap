## Web-based analysis and training

- <a href="https://ai-sensei.com/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">AI Sensei</a> — review games with AI at your level.
- <a href="https://kifubara.app/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Kifubara</a>
- <a href="https://maksimkorzh.github.io/go/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Go AI sparring partner</a> — aimed at single-digit kyu and low-dan players.
- <a href="https://playweiqi.net/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">PlayWeiqi</a> — study with AI in the browser.
- <a href="https://golens.org/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">GoLens</a> — analyse mistakes and learn from them.

- <a href="https://321go.org/home/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">321Go</a>.
- <a href="https://www.zbaduk.com/tools" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">ZBaduk</a> — similar to AI Sensei.
